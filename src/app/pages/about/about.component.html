THE PERSON BEHIND THE POSTS

A little about
Matt.

Software, side projects, and a few things that have nothing to do with either.

I've taken a somewhat unconventional path through my career. I've worked in software engineering, sales, and even started my own car dealership.

Most recently, I spent five years at ClickUp, working on its Angular application and helping build the company's design system from the ground up. What started with two engineers and a handful of components grew into a shared foundation used by more than 250 developers.

That was some of my favorite work. I particularly enjoyed the challenge of designing components that could meet the needs of different teams without becoming difficult to use or maintain. Seeing something we'd built become part of hundreds of developers' everyday work was pretty rewarding.

Before that, I worked in sales and founded EVLabz, a car dealership specializing in Teslas. It's a pretty different world from software engineering, but one I enjoyed being part of. There's something rewarding about building a business from scratch and seeing people choose something you've created.

I started MeetMattZ to have somewhere to share my work and ideas. Some posts will get into Angular, TypeScript, component architecture, and the decisions behind the code. Others might cover a personal project, something I've built, or an experiment that seemed worth sharing.

Outside of software, I enjoy working on my motorcycle, designing and 3D printing parts, cooking, traveling, and getting some exercise. I also read a lot of nonfiction, usually on whatever subject has caught my interest lately.

Some of what I share here will be useful. Some of it will just be fun. Ideally, both.
