After solving a problem, it’s tempting to close the tabs and move on. The details feel obvious now. Surely they’ll still be obvious next month.

Often, they aren’t. The missing context is usually the useful part: what you tried first, what surprised you, and which assumption turned out to be wrong.

A useful technical note can be short. Describe the problem, explain the approach that worked, and include the reason behind the decision. Write for someone who has just reached the same confusing spot.

That someone might be a teammate, a stranger searching for an answer, or you six months from now. All three are good reasons to leave a note.
