There’s something nice about having a small corner of the internet that’s your own. No feed to keep up with, no algorithm to figure out. Just a place to write and a link to share.

That’s the idea behind MeetMattZ. A simple developer’s notebook for the things I build, the problems I work through, and the ideas I want to come back to.

The plan is to keep things small. Write when there’s something worth sharing. Let the site grow a little at a time. A useful note doesn’t have to be a definitive guide; sometimes it’s enough to explain one thing clearly.

This is a starter post for the new blog. More notes will find their way here as the site takes shape. For now, welcome to the beginning.
